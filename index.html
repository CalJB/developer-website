About

I've always loved making things and figuring out how things work. Web development happened to be the first place where those two interests collided. HTML gave me a way to build things, while programming gave me the puzzles I enjoyed solving.

I first discovered that combination when my dad taught me HTML as a kid. I kept coming back to it over the years, experimenting with CSS, JavaScript, Python, and anything else that caught my attention.

These days I'm turning that lifelong curiosity into a career, building my skills across the stack and learning by actually making things.

I'm currently building towards a well rounded junior web development skillset, with a particular interest in backend development. I'm working through the fundamentals of modern web development while building projects that give me a chance to practice them in the real world.

Right now, that means getting comfortable with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, Git, databases, APIs, deployment, and the less glamorous but equally important parts of maintaining a website.
